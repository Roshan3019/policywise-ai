# Car Insurance Knowledge Base

## No Claim Bonus (NCB)

No Claim Bonus is a discount offered to policyholders who do not make any claims during the policy year. It rewards safe driving and claim-free years.

### How NCB Works:
- **2nd year**: 20% discount on Own Damage (OD) premium
- **3rd year**: 25% discount on OD premium
- **4th year**: 35% discount on OD premium
- **5th year onwards**: 45% discount on OD premium (maximum)

### Important Points:
- NCB is only applicable to Own Damage (OD) premium, not Third Party (TP) premium
- NCB is transferable when you switch insurance companies
- NCB can be claimed only if the policy is renewed within 90 days of expiry

## Zero Depreciation Cover

Zero Depreciation is an add-on cover that allows you to claim the full value of plastic, rubber, and metal parts without any depreciation deduction.

### Coverage:
- Plastic parts: 50% depreciation normally, but 0% with this cover
- Rubber parts: 50% depreciation normally, but 0% with this cover
- Metal parts: 0% depreciation applies

### Who Should Buy:
- New car owners (first 2-3 years)
- Vehicles driven frequently in city traffic
- High-end cars with expensive parts

## IDV (Insured Declared Value)

IDV is the maximum amount you will receive if your car is stolen or completely damaged beyond repair. It represents the current market value of your car.

### IDV Calculation:
- Based on manufacturer's listed price
- Depreciation is applied based on the car's age
- Age-based depreciation rates:
  - 0-6 months: 5% depreciation
  - 6 months - 1 year: 15% depreciation
  - 1-2 years: 20% depreciation
  - 2-3 years: 30% depreciation
  - 3-4 years: 40% depreciation
  - 4-5 years: 50% depreciation
  - Above 5 years: Based on mutual agreement

## Engine Protection Cover

Engine Protection is an add-on cover that protects against damage to the engine due to water ingress, oil leakage, or other mechanical failures.

### Covered Situations:
- Hydrostatic lock (engine seizing due to water entry)
- Engine damage due to oil sump leakage
- Gearbox and differential damage

### Exclusions:
- Normal wear and tear
- Lack of maintenance
- Racing or motorsports activities

## Keyless Entry/Exit Cover

This add-on covers damages to the keyless entry system, remote key, and related electronic components.

### Coverage Includes:
- Remote key replacement
- Key fob repair/replacement
- Programming costs
- Lock mechanism repairs

## Roadside Assistance

Roadside assistance provides 24x7 emergency support during vehicle breakdowns.

### Services Provided:
- Flat tire change
- Fuel delivery (up to 10 liters)
- Battery jump-start
- Lockout service (key duplication)
- Towing service (up to 50 km)

## Claim Process

### Step-by-Step Process:
1. **Inform the insurer**: Notify within 48 hours of the incident
2. **Submit documents**: FIR copy, driving license, RC, insurance documents
3. **Surveyor inspection**: Surveyor assesses vehicle damage
4. **Approval**: Claim is approved after surveyor report
5. **Cashless settlement**: If network garage, repair without paying upfront
6. **Reimbursement**: If non-network garage, pay first, claim later
